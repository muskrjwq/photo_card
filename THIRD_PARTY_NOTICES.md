[VERLab/accelerated_features](https://github.com/verlab/accelerated_features).

Paper: *XFeat: Accelerated Features for Lightweight Image Matching*, CVPR 2024  
Authors: Guilherme Potje, Felipe Cadar, Andre Araujo, Renato Martins, Erickson R. Nascimento

also licensed under Apache License 2.0. Its license text is preserved at
`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/XFeat-ONNX-Sample-Apache-2.0.txt`.

locally in the visitor's browser.
